## Required production files

Before the visual system is treated as final production branding, obtain:

1. Official primary and secondary logo files in SVG and transparent PNG.
2. Approved horizontal, stacked, monochrome and small-size logo lockups.
3. Official colour values for RGB, HEX, CMYK and print use.
4. Official typeface names, licences, weights and fallback rules.
5. Approved horizon/wave and dot-pattern source artwork.
6. Photography, illustration and iconography guidance.
7. Cultural content approval workflow and named approvers.
8. Minimum logo size, exclusion zone and prohibited-use guidance.

## Non-invention boundary

The CSS circle, ray, dot and horizon elements are neutral geometric placeholders derived from observable layout properties. They do not claim cultural meaning and must not be described as traditional, ceremonial, symbolic or community-approved until KOTC formally confirms that usage.
