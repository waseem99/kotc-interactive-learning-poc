# Proposal Demo Script

Recommended duration: 6–8 minutes.

## 1. Establish the boundary

Open `/` and state that this is a functional proof of concept demonstrating interaction, accessibility, responsive behaviour, and maintainability. It is not the final cultural design.

## 2. Show account and role logic

Open `/sign-in`, switch between sign-in and create-account modes, and explain that production authentication will use managed identity and privacy controls. Continue to `/role-selection` and select the trades-learner pathway.

## 3. Show progress and learner control

On `/dashboard`, highlight:

- Saved role
- Overall progress
- Continue-learning priority
- Module states
- Certificate status
- Theme, text-size, and reduced-motion controls

Refresh the page to demonstrate local persistence.

## 4. Demonstrate the interactive house

Open `/house` and:

- Select multiple rooms/topics
- Explain current, available, and completed states
- Navigate using keyboard
- Switch to the accessible list view
- Explain that final imagery and content will be KOTC-led

## 5. Demonstrate learning interactions

Open the lesson and show:

- Media controls
- Captions and transcript
- Downloadable-resource concept
- Click-and-flip cards
- Scroll-to-highlight sequence

## 6. Complete the accessible activity

Open the activity and demonstrate both methods:

- Drag a material card into a category
- Select another card and use “Place selected here”
- Submit an incorrect arrangement once to show supportive feedback
- Correct the arrangement and open the completion state

## 7. Show administration and maintainability

Open `/admin` and show illustrative analytics, content status, and text-supported charts. Open `/admin/content-editor` to demonstrate structured content, captions/transcripts, activity authoring, cultural/accessibility review, and publishing states.

## 8. Close on delivery credibility

State that the POC frontend is designed to connect to the proposed NestJS, Strapi, PostgreSQL, Cognito, and AWS Canada production architecture.
