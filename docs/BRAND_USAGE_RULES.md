# KOTC Brand V3 Usage Rules

## Purpose

These rules govern the proposal-stage Brand V3 implementation until KOTC supplies official brand standards. They preserve recognizability, accessibility and cultural safety.

## Colour hierarchy

- White or a very soft neutral must remain the dominant canvas.
- Deep navy is the primary hierarchy and action colour.
- Pale sky blue identifies contextual learning support and selected content.
- Golden yellow marks active position, progress, focus and limited decorative emphasis.
- Warm red is a secondary accent for milestones, pathways and controlled brand punctuation.
- Green is reserved for success states only and must not dominate any screen.
- Yellow and red must not be used as small body text on white.
- Meaning must never depend on colour alone.

## Geometric motifs

- Circle and ray treatments may be used as generic visual geometry only.
- Dot texture may appear only in decorative navy regions and never behind body text, form fields or controls.
- Layered horizon bands may be used in the hero, completion state, section transitions and footer.
- Decorative treatments must simplify or disappear on mobile and at high zoom.
- No cultural meaning may be assigned to a motif without written KOTC approval.

## Typography

- Use the approved KOTC typeface when supplied.
- Until then, use the humanist sans fallback stack defined in `brand-v3-tokens.css`.
- Headings should feel confident and friendly, not editorial or luxury-oriented.
- Body content should use a readable line length of approximately 680–720 pixels.
- Do not use all caps for long labels or paragraphs.

## Components

### Buttons

- One visually dominant primary action per task area.
- Primary: navy background, white text.
- Secondary: white background, navy border and text.
- Gold may appear as focus or active emphasis but not as a full primary-button background with white text.

### Cards and panels

- Use spacing and borders before shadows.
- Sky-blue panels are reserved for support, transcripts, contextual guidance and selected learning.
- Gold and red accents should normally appear as borders, markers or small graphic areas.
- Avoid grids of equal cards when one action or story is clearly more important.

### Progress and status

- Progress uses navy structure and gold current-position markers.
- Completed status must include text or icon, not green alone.
- Draft, review, accessibility review, cultural review and approval states require explicit labels.

## Accessibility

- All interactive states must satisfy WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- Controls require a visible keyboard focus indicator.
- Pointer, touch and keyboard routes must provide equivalent outcomes.
- The interactive house must retain a first-class structured-list alternative.
- Reduced-motion mode must remove nonessential transitions and decorative animation.
- Layouts must remain usable at 200% zoom and with large/extra-large text settings.

## Cultural and content approval

- Do not invent Indigenous language, stories, patterns, teachings, symbols, medicines, animals or ceremonial references.
- Do not label generic geometry as Indigenous or culturally significant.
- All final imagery, wording, cultural references and production brand assets require KOTC review.
- Placeholder content must remain clearly identified until approved.

## Prohibited treatments

- Green-heavy sustainability-template styling.
- Pastel editorial serif direction.
- Decorative motifs behind form controls or body copy.
- Yellow or red small text on white.
- Unapproved logo reconstruction presented as official artwork.
- Cultural symbolism inferred from a screenshot.
- Dense dashboards made from visually equal cards.
