# KOTC POC Editorial Design System v0.2

## Purpose

This visual system gives the bid-stage proof of concept a premium, contemporary and architectural character while preserving the existing learner journey, accessibility controls and cultural-safety boundaries.

It is a functional proposal direction, not a final KOTC cultural identity. Final imagery, terminology, symbolism and brand treatment remain subject to KOTC-led co-design, community input and formal approval.

## Experience direction

The interface should feel:

- warm and editorial rather than institutional;
- spacious rather than card-dense;
- connected to construction, materials and learning;
- contemporary without becoming decorative;
- accessible without looking clinical;
- culturally cautious and easy to replace after co-design.

## Colour roles

| Role | Token | Value | Usage |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Page canvas | `--color-page` | `#F7F4EE` | Warm ivory background |
| Main surface | `--color-card` | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and content surfaces |
| Secondary surface | `--color-card-soft` | `#EEE9E1` | Stone panels and grouped content |
| Primary text | `--color-text` | `#191A18` | Headings and high-emphasis copy |
| Secondary text | `--color-text-soft` | `#66645F` | Supporting copy |
| Primary action | `--color-primary` | `#314A62` | Main calls to action and navigation |
| Expressive accent | `--color-accent` | `#B85F45` | Clay highlights, selection and editorial emphasis |
| Supporting accent | `--color-warning` | `#9A6A23` | Ochre information and review states |
| Secondary accent | `--color-secondary` | `#7C5A73` | Plum pathway and data-visualization use |
| Completion only | `--color-success` | `#47705C` | Completed states and positive feedback |

Green is intentionally reserved for completion and success. It is not the page atmosphere, primary action colour or dominant brand treatment.

## Typography

- Display and editorial headings: Source Serif 4 when available, with robust system-serif fallbacks.
- Interface and learning content: Atkinson Hyperlegible when available, with system sans-serif fallbacks.
- Large serif headings are reserved for major page statements and section introductions.
- Dashboards, tables, forms and controls remain sans-serif.
- Reading content remains approximately 700px wide.

## Spatial system

- Desktop sections: approximately 92–140px vertical space.
- Mobile sections: approximately 64–92px vertical space.
- Main cards: 24–40px internal spacing.
- Major content groups use whitespace and divider lines before adding containers.
- Cards of equal visual weight are avoided where a clear feature hierarchy is possible.

## Surface hierarchy

1. Ivory page canvas.
2. White primary content surfaces.
3. Stone, blue, clay, ochre and plum soft surfaces for controlled emphasis.
4. Charcoal or deep-blue high-emphasis feature surfaces.
5. Green surfaces only for completed or successful states.

## Component direction

### Header

- White, lightly translucent surface.
- Text navigation with clay active underline.
- Charcoal brand mark.
- Display preferences consolidated into one accessible menu.

### Buttons

- Deep-blue primary actions.
- White secondary actions with visible borders.
- Soft rectangular corners rather than pill treatment everywhere.
- Clay used for editorial emphasis, not as a universal action colour.

### Homepage

- Ivory and white hero with a controlled clay architectural plane.
- One dominant interface composition instead of many floating objects.
- Asymmetrical capability layout with one featured requirement.

### Learner dashboard

- One dominant continue-learning area.
- Progress, current chapter and next action receive the strongest hierarchy.
- Module lists use divider lines rather than repeated heavy cards.

### Interactive house

- Architectural-grid canvas.
- Charcoal line structure and clay roof.
- Controlled room accents: stone, clay, blue, ochre, white and plum.
- Completed states use subtle green treatment.
- Selected state uses clay outline.
- Equivalent list view remains available.

### Lesson and activities

- Editorial reading layout with larger whitespace.
- Deep-blue media environment.
- Stone callouts with clay edge treatment.
- Material cards use sample-board styling and multi-accent categories.
- Drag-and-drop and select-and-place remain functionally equivalent.

### Administrator and CMS

- White surfaces, thin dividers and restrained shadows.
- Multi-colour charts using blue, clay, ochre and plum.
- Green reserved for approved/completed states.
- Publishing workflow shown as a vertical timeline.

## Motion

- Motion supports orientation, selection and feedback.
- Hover elevation is limited to a few pixels.
- No parallax, autoplay decoration or camera-like movement.
- Reduced-motion preference remains fully supported.

## Accessibility

The redesign preserves the WCAG 2.2 Level AA target and must retain:

- visible focus indicators;
- logical keyboard order;
- 200% text zoom support;
- narrow-screen reflow;
- non-drag activity alternatives;
- interactive-house list alternative;
- captions and transcript concept;
- text equivalents for charts;
- status announcements for activity feedback;
- colour-independent state communication.

## Cultural-safety boundary

The design may use neutral architectural diagrams, material textures and construction-related interface iconography. It must not invent or appropriate Indigenous motifs, patterns, stories, language, sacred items or community-specific visual claims.
